Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

Are these fixed prices?

No. These are starting prices for straightforward setup work. You will receive a scope and quote based on the devices, complexity, site conditions and estimated labor before scheduling.

Does the price include TVs, speakers, routers or other equipment?

No. Equipment, accessories, cables and third-party contractor work are quoted separately. We can discuss equipment you already have and recommend options.

Can I combine all three packages?

Yes. Whole-home projects bring indoor, office and outdoor technology into one coordinated quote, with no duplicate charges for the same work.

Will you mount my outdoor TV or install security cameras?

We can help plan, configure and connect eligible consumer devices. Mounting, electrical work, new wiring, permanently installed audio and regulated security or alarm systems may need a properly licensed and insured installer.

Do you offer weekend appointments?

Yes, weekend appointments are available subject to scheduling. Share your preferred dates when you request a quote.

Do you provide small-business IT support?

Yes. We can discuss separately scoped small-business IT projects and provide an individual estimate.
